Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026 · Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy describes how PE Smart ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our iOS application. By installing or using PE Smart you agree to the terms set out below.

Short version: PE Smart does not collect, transmit, store on a server, or sell any personal or financial data. Everything you enter into the App stays on your device, inside your iOS sandbox.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PE Smart is engineered around a privacy-by-default model. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Collect financial account numbers, brokerage credentials, or bank login details.
  • Collect biometric data, contacts, calendars, photos (beyond what you explicitly select for scanning), or location.
  • Track your behaviour across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Send analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or device identifiers to any server.
  • Display advertising of any kind.

2. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device, encrypted inside the iOS application sandbox. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Portfolio and asset records (symbol, quantity, average cost, current price).
  • Transaction history (date, type, quantity, price, fee).
  • Budget definitions and expense entries.
  • Goal definitions and contribution history.
  • Scanned or imported document images.
  • Notes entered into the Secure Notes vault.
  • User preferences (theme, appearance).

You can permanently delete this data at any time from Settings → Reset Demo Data inside the App, or by deleting the App from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no social-login SDKs, and no remote data-collection endpoints. Charts and visualizations are rendered natively in Swift using Core Animation.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because the App does not collect any information from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users.

6. International Transfers

Because no data leaves your device, there are no international data transfers to disclose.

7. Your Rights

Because no personal data is collected, transmitted, or stored by us, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, or delete on your behalf. You retain full control over the data on your device through standard iOS app-management tools.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable regulations. Material updates will be visible in the App and on this page, with the "Last updated" date revised at the top. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
