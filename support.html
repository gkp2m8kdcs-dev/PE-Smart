Quick answers

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device, inside the iOS app sandbox. PE Smart does not sync to any cloud or server.

How do I back up my portfolios?

Use Settings → Export Snapshot to share a text summary, or back up your iPhone with iCloud / iTunes — the App's data is included in standard iOS device backups.

How do I reset demo data?

Open Tools → Settings → Reset Demo Data. This clears all portfolios, budgets, goals, notes, and scanned documents from the device.

Are prices real-time?

PE Smart generates simulated price history to drive its charts and analytics. It is not a brokerage or trading platform and does not connect to live market feeds.

Can I import holdings from my broker?

Manual entry is supported today. Add assets one at a time from the Portfolio tab with symbol, quantity, average cost, and current price.

Why doesn't the App request notification permission?

Notifications are not needed for PE Smart to function. The App deliberately keeps the permission surface to camera and photos only.
